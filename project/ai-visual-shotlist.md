# SurvivalBase · AI visual shot list

All product photos are generated from scratch with BytePlus Seedream (coupon credits), using the supplier photo only as a product reference (`image` input), never as the final image.

## Art direction (applies to every shot)

- **Look:** cinematic, natural light, muted earth palette that matches the brand (ink #1F1B16, paper #F2EDE2, signal orange #D9692B as an accent only). Slight film grain, shallow depth of field.
- **Setting:** northern Europe — Belgian Ardennes woods, a Brussels flat during a blackout, a garage, a cellar shelf. Weather: overcast, rain, dusk, night with torchlight.
- **People:** hands and partial figures, practical clothing, no faces in close-up, no weapons, no military styling.
- **Product fidelity:** keep shape, colours, logos and proportions identical to the reference photo. No invented features.
- **Format:** 1:1 for shop cards (2048×2048), 4:5 for the product page, 16:9 for zone headers.

## Three shots per product

1. **In use** — the product doing its job in a real situation (hero image).
2. **How it works** — 3-step sequence in one image (triptych, left to right), each step a clear action. Small step numbers 1-2-3 in the brand mono font can be added afterwards in layout, not in the generation.
3. **Specs** — clean still life on raw concrete or a canvas tarp, product shown from 3/4 with the details that matter visible (ports, valves, buckle, blade lock…). Callout lines and numbers are added afterwards in layout.
